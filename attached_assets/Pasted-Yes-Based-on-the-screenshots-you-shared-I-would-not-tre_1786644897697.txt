Yes. Based on the screenshots you shared, I would **not** treat this as a simple “UI redesign.” I would redesign the application around a clear **user journey and workspace model**.

Your current UI is trying to make one screen handle library management, chapter management, reading, listening, and audiobook generation. That is why buttons, metadata, and actions are competing for space.

The new model should be:

> **Discover → Open Book → Resume → Read / Listen → Navigate Chapters → Convert when needed → Track progress → Return later**

The application should feel like a **book reader + audiobook player + audiobook creation studio**, rather than an admin dashboard.

---
